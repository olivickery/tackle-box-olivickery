Getting started

Five quick steps to get the most out of it.
1
 Add your first piece of gear. Tap the yellow +, snap the packet, and the brand, model, colour and specs fill in for you.
2
 Star your go-to lures. Favourites sit at the top, and the filter pills get you to them in one tap.
3
 Plan your next trip. Tap Plan, say where you’re fishing and what you’re after, and get tactics, a loadout from your own gear and the gaps worth filling.
4
 Pack from a checklist. Turn the plan into a pack list, tick gear off as it goes in the bag, and save it for next time.
5
 Put it on your home screen. On iPhone, open tackleboxiq.com in Safari, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the Chrome menu.
 

What it does
📷

Photo inventory

Up to four photos per item. Swipe through them and pinch to zoom in on the fine print.
🪄

Packet scanning

Photograph the packaging and the brand, model, colour, specs and target species are filled in for you.
✨

AI trip planner

Tell it where you’re fishing, what you’re targeting and the conditions. You get tactics, a loadout from your own gear and the gaps worth filling.
📋

Pack lists

Tick gear off as you pack, add anything from your tackle box, and save lists by trip, species or date.
🛍

Restock list

Lost a lure? One tap adds it. Find it on Amazon, or share the list with a link.
⭐

Favourites at a glance

Star your go-to gear and filter to favourites or items to replace in one tap.
🔍

Find anything fast

Live search across brand, colour, specs, species and location.
📱

Built for your phone

Made for the boat, the bank and the car park, and it syncs to desktop too.
